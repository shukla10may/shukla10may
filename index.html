About Us

UPTEC Computer Consultancy Ltd. - a premier IT company based in North India- was established in 1993 as a joint venture with UP Electronics Corporation Limited, a public sector enterprise. UPTEC is a brainchild of a group of seasoned IT professionals and management gurus with rich experience in world-renowned organizations such as IIMs, IITs, Bell Labs (USA), IBM, among others, and a passion for excellence. Over the three decades since its inception, UPTEC has expanded, diversified, and emerged as a total IT solutions company with abiding commitment to professionalism and impeccable quality. UPTEC has so far developed more than 2,00,000 I.T. professionals working in India and abroad.
